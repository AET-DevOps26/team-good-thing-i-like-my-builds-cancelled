# 📝 Problem Statement: Travel Journal Application

Many people enjoy traveling in Germany. Keeping track of the places one has visited, the sights one has seen, and the experiences 
one has gained may get overwhelming. Discovering sights one may have missed, or finding new destinations to explore, 
that also match one's interests, can be difficult and time-consuming.

## Main Functionality

The Travel Journal Application will support multiple key features:
- Users can Keep Track of their trips. The following information will be stored for each trip:
  - When was the Trip, and how long did it last
  - What was the destination
  - How did I travel to and from the destination
- Users can write descriptions of their trips and can use AI to help them write smooth and consistent travel reports
- Users can get Recommendations for sights to explore along their routes, based on past trips
- Users can plan their train journeys for their trips by using the application's planning feature. They select a start and destination, 
and the application will provide them with a list of possible train connections, including estimated travel times and number of train transitions. 
The user can then select the best connection for their trip.
- Once they decided on a train connection, the application will provide them with a list of sights to explore along their route. 
The user can then include these suggestions in their travel reports.

## Intended Users

The intended users of the Travel Journal Application are private individuals who enjoy traveling and want to keep track 
of their trips and discover new destinations. A certain liking for trains and statistics may be a common trait among the users, but it is not a requirement.

## AI Integration

The Travel Journal Application will integrate AI to assist users in writing smooth and consistent travel reports of the
sights they have visited. The AI will further provide recommendations for sights to explore along their routes. Therefore, similar past trips will be taken into account.


## Use Case Scenarios
Jane is currently on a Trip to Marburg. This has been on her Bucket list for years. Now that she is finally there, 
she wants to keep track of her trip and document it. She uses the Travel Journal Application to do so. She specifies
the route she has taken (what train, when, ...), and writes descriptions of her experiences. The AI helps her to 
write nice travel reports.

Steward has been on many Trips to Berlin, Hamburg, Frankfurt, and Bremen, where he visited Museums about modern art. He uses the Travel Journal 
Application to keep track of his trips. His next trip is to Munich, the AI analyzes his past trips and recommends a few museums to visit in Munich,
which Steward includes in his travel report.

Alice doesn't like to travel by train. However, she still wants to document them in the Travel Journal app.
She can still use the application to log her travels, and even specify different modes of transportation. While she 
doesn't use the train planning feature, she can still use the AI to help her write travel reports.
